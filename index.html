Kitchen Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Kitchen Studio ("we", "us") is a personal cookbook app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

  • Your cookbook is stored on your phone. You can use Kitchen Studio without an account.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, we use your email address to sign you in.
  • Cloud backup is off unless it is switched on for your version of the app.
  • We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your information.
  • You can delete your account, and the data stored with it, from inside the app.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves the following on your phone: your cookbook's name and design, your cooking preferences (such as units, servings, dietary needs and foods to avoid), your recipes, notes and memories, photos you add, and your grocery list and meal plan. We can't see this information. It is removed when you delete it in the app or delete the app.

Account information (optional)

You can sign in with a link sent to your email address. If you do, we receive and keep your email address and an account ID created by our sign-in service. We use them only to sign you in and run your account. We don't send marketing emails.

Cloud backup (only when switched on)

When cloud backup is switched on and you are signed in, a copy of your cookbook, preferences, recipes, notes, memories, grocery list and photos is saved to your account so it can be restored to your phone. Your meal plan is not backed up. Backed-up data can only be read or changed by your account. Photos are kept in private storage and are not available at public web addresses.

Importing and sharing recipes

  • When you import a recipe from a link, your phone opens that web page to read the recipe, as a web browser would. The website sees an ordinary visit from your phone. We don't receive the link or the page. A photo kept from a website may be loaded from that website when you view the recipe.
  • On iPhone, when you open Add recipe, the app checks whether you have copied a link so it can offer to import it. It reads the link only if you tap to import it.
  • When you share a link or text to Kitchen Studio from another app, only what you share is passed to the app. When you share from Kitchen Studio, such as your grocery list, it goes through your phone's share sheet to the people and apps you choose.

Camera and photos

The app asks for your permission before using your camera or photo library, and uses them only when you choose to add a picture. You can change this at any time in your phone's Settings.

Service providers

We don't use advertising or analytics services. These providers help us run the app and may use information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase provides sign-in, the account database and private photo storage. It receives your email address and account ID and, if cloud backup is on, your backed-up data. Like most online services, it keeps short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security. Account data is stored in the European Union.
  • Expo delivers app updates. When the app checks for an update, it sends basic technical details such as the app version and device type. It doesn't receive your cookbook content.
  • Apple distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight. Apple's privacy policy applies to downloads and to feedback or crash reports you choose to send through TestFlight.

Security

Sign-in details on your phone are kept in the iPhone Keychain. Information sent to our service providers is encrypted in transit, and backed-up data is restricted to your account. No system is completely secure, so we collect only what the app needs.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • On your phone: kept until you delete it in the app (or choose Start again) or delete the app.
  • Your account and backup: kept until you delete your account. To do this, open The Cook tab and tap Delete account. This removes your account, your backed-up data and your stored photos. If you can't use the app, email us and we will delete your account for you.
  • Technical logs held by service providers are deleted automatically after a short period.

Your rights

We use your information to provide the features you ask for and to keep the service secure. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, email team@kitchenstudioapp.co. We will reply within one month. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

Children

Kitchen Studio is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. For important changes, we will let you know before they take effect, for example in the app or by email.
